Specializing in Non-Destructive Testing for industrial inspection, with hands-on experience in Ultrasonic Testing (UT), Penetrant Testing (PT), and Magnetic Particle Testing (MT).

UT uses ultrasonic waves to identify internal and subsurface discontinuities. PT reveals surface-breaking defects using a visible or fluorescent penetrant, while MT uses magnetic fields to identify surface and near-surface indications in ferromagnetic materials.
